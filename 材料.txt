演讲稿初稿

Title: My Journey of Learning English

主讲:
Good morning, dear teachers and classmates.
It’s my great honor to come back to our high school and share my English learning journey with you all.

English is a global language. It opens a window for us to know the world. We can read foreign articles, watch original videos and communicate with people from different countries with it. For us students, it is also an important subject for further study.

When I started learning English, I met lots of troubles. I was poor at remembering new words, and I was afraid of speaking English in public. I worried that I would make mistakes and be laughed at. Listening exercises were also hard for me — I could not catch the key information quickly.

“刚才我提到的这些痛点，相信在座的各位学弟学妹都深有体会。既然有问题，咱们就要对症下药。接下来，我请出我的几位‘外援’，给大家分享一些实用的提分秘籍。”

念稿人 A（补充听力/阅读技巧）：念群A的笔记，用中文快速讲（不要超过1分钟）。“听力要精听，文本里模糊的单词就是罪魁祸首；阅读先求准度，再提速度。”

控场：“说到阅读提速，完形填空可是重灾区。我们来玩个扫码抢答，大家猜猜大型考试里，完形ABCD选项比例通常是多少？” -> 互动后，请出念稿人B。

念稿人 B（补充完形/语法）：用中文讲群A补充的终极秘籍（约1.5分钟）。“大型考试完形ABCD比例通常是4:4:4:3；语法填空考11个高频点，动词/形容词/名词频率最高，冠词一般就一个。”

念稿人 C（补充写作技巧）：用中文讲群A红笔框出的写作技巧（约1分钟）。“读后续写先列大纲！150词分2段，不要跑偏，字数不要超。”

念稿人 D（补充素养/背词法）：用中文讲群A和群B的长期积累法（约1分钟）。“素养方面，去听歌、看《蜘蛛侠》全英版；背单词每次200个分一组，循环7天，考前两周专攻难词。”

主讲:
听了刚才同学们的干货，大家是不是觉得豁然开朗？其实，我也是在用这些方法，慢慢地我有了进步……

Later, I tried some useful ways to change this. Instead of reciting word lists mechanically, I learned words in sentences and short stories.Just like what we just shared, I kept listening to English podcasts, and even watched English movie clips, every day for 15 to 20 minutes. And I told myself not to fear mistakes. I tried to read English texts aloud and speak out my ideas bravely.

Slowly, I made progress. Now I can understand simple English materials and express myself more confidently. But I still have problems. For example, I need to improve my writing and learn more advanced vocabulary.

Here are some suggestions for you. First and foremost, keep practicing every day. English needs long-term accumulation, not cramming before exams. In addition, don’t be afraid of making mistakes. Mistakes help us find our weak points. Last but not the least， try to make English part of your daily life.

You can listen to English songs or watch short English clips in your free time.
Learning English is not easy, but it is rewarding. Take it step by step, and you will see your progress one day.

Thank you for your listening！

我的英语学习之旅
早上好，亲爱的老师和同学们。
非常荣幸回到母校，和大家分享我的英语学习之路。

学习英语并不轻松，但付出总会有回报。一步一步坚持下去，终有一天你会看到进步。
