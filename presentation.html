Where I got stuck

Five problems I used to have

1Listening: one lost minute, and the whole passage collapsed.听力一走神，整段就崩。
2Reading and seven-choose-five: accuracy came late, so time ran out.做不完，最后几分钟靠猜。
3Cloze: I missed the story, then wrong answers snowballed.没读懂，一个错接着一个错。
4Word forms: I knew the word but could not write the right form.词认识，变形写不出。
5Writing: the prompt was there, the first sentence was not.作文憋半天，开不了头。
【第4页｜主讲｜约60秒】\n逐条念，先讲现象再讲原因：多数不是“我不行”，是方法、积累和复盘的问题。最后落到“词汇是根，但只背词表不够”。

【第5页｜主讲｜约50秒】\n先给基本盘，再补一句“今天不讲小学初中，聚焦高中：一边讲考试技巧，一边讲英语素养”。
Listening

Blurred words sink the whole passage

1Listen first — do not translate while the audio is running.边听边翻译，注意力就断了。
2Read the transcript after every set: mark blurred words, linking and weak forms.模糊词、连读弱读，全都标出来。
3Shadow 15–20 minutes a day; copy the rhythm, not just the words.每天跟读一刻钟，比考前突击有用。
【第6页｜场控｜约70秒】\n先抛一句“听不懂全文不要紧，怕的是文本里有模糊词”。三步讲完，强调“回看文本”和“跟读”，不要只刷题。
Reading

Accuracy first, speed second

1Get the answers right before you chase the clock.先做对，再逐渐限时。
2Split long sentences: subject–verb–object, connectors, modifiers.长难句拆成主谓宾、连接词、修饰成分。
3For every wrong answer, go back and locate the evidence.错题回文定位，别只看答案。
4Review beats volume: ten analysed passages beat thirty skimmed ones.复盘十篇，强过囫囵刷三十篇。
【第7页｜主讲｜约60秒】\n节奏“先准后快”。讲错题定位时举例：是没读懂、被干扰项骗了，还是时间不够，三种错法对应三种改法。
Cloze

Two passes, one story

1First pass: read the whole story for plot and feeling.先通读，抓人物、情节、情绪。
2Second pass: fill each blank with grammar and context, not gut feeling.第二遍扣细节，语法加语境。
34:4:4:3 is a check, not a guessing trick.大型考试常见 4:4:4:3，但这是经验规律，不是官方规定。做完用它检查分布，别拿它蒙答案。

Most cloze passages are stories. Ask why the character acts that way — empathy is a reading skill.
完形多是故事，共情也是阅读能力。

【第8页｜场控｜约70秒】\n两遍法讲清楚，再专门拆 4:4:4:3：不是蒙题口诀，是做完后的检查工具。理解不到位，才会一个错接着一个错。
Grammar fill-in · 语法填空

Part of speech first, then the form

noun plurals · 名词单复数 tenses · 各类时态 ordinals · 序数词 articles · 冠词 linking words · 逻辑连接词 passive voice · 被动语态 non-finite verbs · 非谓语 appositives · 同位语 three clauses · 三大从句 subjunctive · 虚拟语气 inversion · 倒装
1Learn the rule through one standard example.理清规则，用标准例句吃透。
2Write five sentences of your own, in five different scenes.自己仿写 5 个不同场景的句子。
3Collect exam-ready patterns — and actually write them out.整理万能句式，必须动笔；只看懂等于没掌握。
【第9页｜主讲｜约70秒】\n这页信息密，中文讲：先判词性（名、动、形、副、代、连、冠），再想变形。11 个考点是高频，不是每年绝对固定；冠词“一般一个”也是经验。
Writing

Outline first, direction first

1Spend two minutes on a three-line outline. 先列提纲，写的时候思路才清。
2Continuation writing: choose the direction before you write a word.续写先定方向，不然容易跑偏。
3150 words, two paragraphs — do not overshoot the limit.150 词写 2 段，别超字数。
4Templates are a floor, not a script. Adapt them to the prompt.模板保底，但别死套。
【第10页｜场控｜约60秒】\n先讲小作文提纲，再讲读后续写：动作、情绪、环境三类万能句各备两句，考前一定动笔仿写。
Vocabulary · 背词法

200 words, 7 days, one loop

1Collect unknown words from papers and practice sets — not just the word list.从试卷和刷题里收生词，别只从 abandon 开始。
2Group 200 words; weaker learners can use 100 or 50.每 200 个一组；基础弱就减到 100 或 50。
3Cycle one group for 7 days, then review on schedule.一组连续循环 7 天，遗忘后及时复盘。
4Keep a hard-word list; three misses means a pre-exam sprint.三次记不住的归为难词，考前两周集中冲刺。
【第11页｜主讲｜约60秒】\n中文讲循环：关键不是一次背多少，是分组、循环、复盘、语境。200 只是参考值。
English outside the exam

Feed the language, not just the score

1Songs: sing along — you learn melody, phrasing and rhythm.听喜欢的英文歌，跟唱，旋律和表达一起学。
2Short films with clear dialogue: listen for the situation, not every word.看台词清楚的短片，先听情境，不必逐词抠。
3Accept English: five minutes a day beats one long cram.接受英语，每天五分钟，胜过偶尔猛攻。
4English does not end when the exam ends.高考结束，不等于英语学习结束。

Clear dialogue, fast rhythm — good for listening practice.
台词清楚、节奏快，适合磨耳朵。

Takeaway · 带得走的东西

Four things I wish I had known

1Practice a little every day — cramming is not a plan.每天少量练习，别把希望押在考前。
2Feed yourself real English, not only textbook exercises.多输入真实、地道的材料。
3Speak and write before you feel ready; mistakes are data, not verdicts.不怕犯错，错误是信息，不是判决。
4Make English part of your life, not just your timetable.把英语放进日常，而不是只放进课表。
