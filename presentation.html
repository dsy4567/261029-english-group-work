Why We Freeze

The psychology of speaking a second language

二语习得主题 · 外语焦虑从何而来，又如何打破

6 speakers 15 slides 2 live interactions
【第1页｜主讲 A｜约40秒】\n开场自我介绍 + 抛出主题。语速放慢，标题停顿一拍再念副标题。
Warm-up

“You know the answer.
Your mouth doesn’t.”

Think of the last time you froze in English class — hand halfway up, words gone.

想一想：上一次你在英语课上“卡壳”的瞬间。今天我们讲的，就是那个瞬间。

【第2页｜主讲 A｜约35秒】\n先自己讲一个卡壳的小故事（越具体越好），再把问题抛给全班。停顿3秒，让教室安静一下。
The phenomenon

You know more than you can show

01

Tip of the tongue舌尖现象

The word is “there” — until you try to say it out loud in your second language.

02

Silent period沉默期

Beginners listen for months before they speak. Silence is part of learning, not a failure.

03

Two systems双系统

Your L1 runs on autopilot. Your L2 borrows the same attention — and attention is limited.

【第3页｜主讲 B｜约60秒】\n三张卡依次展开。01用中文解释“舌尖现象”举英语单词到嘴边说不出的例子；02解释沉默期是正常阶段；03强调注意力资源有限。
Core concept

Foreign Language Anxiety

“A distinct complex of self-perceptions, beliefs, feelings and behaviours related to classroom language learning.”

—— Horwitz, Horwitz & Cope, 1986

Not shyness. Not “bad English”. A specific, measurable anxiety that appears only in the foreign language classroom.
【第4页｜主讲 B｜约50秒】\n引用定义（英文念一遍即可），强调三点：它是可测量的、它专属于语言课堂、它和英语水平没有必然关系——班里英语最好的人也可能最焦虑。
Framework

Three faces of anxiety

A

Communication apprehension交流恐惧

The fear of not being able to speak — going blank when all eyes are on you.

B

Fear of negative evaluation负面评价恐惧

The fear of being judged — classmates noticing every single mistake.

C

Test anxiety测评压力

The fear of being graded — performing worse exactly when it counts.

接下来的自查问卷，就从这三个维度给你打分。

【第5页｜主讲 B｜约55秒】\n每张卡配一个班级里的真实场景（被点名回答、作文被当堂念、考试口语）。结尾一句引出后面自查：“先记住A/B/C三个字母”。
Why it happens

What anxiety does to the brain

1Stress fires up the alarm system (amygdala).焦虑启动大脑的“警报器”。
2Working memory — your mental “workspace” — gets hijacked.工作记忆（大脑的工作台）被占用。
3Less workspace for words → you freeze. It isn’t a knowledge gap.留给词汇的资源变少 → 卡壳。这不是你不会。

Yerkes–Dodson 定律：唤醒水平适中时表现最好，过高时表现急剧下滑。

【第6页｜主讲 C｜约60秒】\n把工作记忆比喻成“手机后台”：焦虑就像同时开了20个App，英语当然卡。用生活化的类比，不要念术语。
Mechanism

The freeze loop

Speak in public→ Heart races, mind watches itself→ Fewer words come out→ “See? I’m bad at this.”→ Avoid the next chance→ …

Anxiety shrinks performance; poor performance feeds anxiety. The loop only breaks when you speak anyway.

恶性循环：越怕越不说，越不说越怕。破环的方法只有一个——先开口，再完美。

【第7页｜主讲 C｜约50秒】\n用手指着一个个chips念，最后停在“…”上。留一个悬念给后面的方法论页：“这个循环怎么破，我们第14页讲”。

【第8页｜主讲 C｜约45秒】\n强调“0”：焦虑藏起你的水平，而不是抹掉你的水平。这句是全场最重要的观点之一，说慢一点。

Debrief

How to read your result

30–

Low低焦虑

You’re comfortable in class. Keep speaking before you feel ready — that’s how it stays.

31–42

Moderate中等焦虑

You can speak, but half your attention watches yourself. That split is what freezes you.

43+

High高焦虑

Fear is doing the talking. Good news: it’s a state, not a verdict — and states can change.

【第10页｜主讲 D｜约50秒】\n重申这不是诊断，是镜子。邀请刚才举手的同学读屏幕里的一句话（自愿）。若无人举手，念自己的结果做示范。
Reset your beliefs

Myths vs facts

MYTH

“Anxiety means I haven’t prepared enough.”

vs
FACT

Preparation helps — but anxiety peaks after you’ve prepared. It’s an emotional loop, not a study problem.

“Fluent people never freeze.”

vs

Even native speakers freeze in public. Fluency reduces the frequency; it doesn’t remove the mechanism.

“I must not make mistakes.”

vs

Mistakes are the visible part of a system being built — interlanguage in motion.

【第11页｜主讲 E｜约60秒】\n左右对照念，节奏是“错—对、错—对、错—对”。第三条为抢答第2题埋伏笔，讲完可以先不说破。
Break the loop

Four things that actually work

1Islands of fluency — prepare 3 key phrases, not a full script.准备三个“语言岛”，而不是整篇稿。
2Lower the stakes — pair practice before whole-class talk.先两人练，再全班说。
3Pause, don’t translate — silence is cheaper than mental translating.用停顿代替心译，沉默比翻译便宜。
4Rehearse under pressure — practise with a timer, then the timer won’t surprise you.限时多练几次，计时就吓不到你。
【第12页｜主讲 E｜约70秒】\n每条给一个中文小场景（课文朗读、口语考试、小组展示）。第4条自然过渡到下一个互动：“说到计时——我们来真的”。
